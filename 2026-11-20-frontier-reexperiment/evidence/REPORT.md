## What this does not show

- **It does not rank the three models.** Twelve tasks, three decodings and one vendor route cannot support a capability ranking, and the task corpus is written by the same people who wrote the graders.
- **Mock artifacts are not evidence.** `--mock` replies are deterministic canned strings keyed by (model, prompt hash). They exercise the plumbing; they say nothing about any model. Every mock report must be regenerated before it is read.
- **It does not measure the obligation grader's correctness.** E2 reports agreement between two checks and the false rejects on hand-written controls. The obligation rules are themselves a program with bugs; a patch graded HOLDS is 'not caught by this rule set', not 'correct'.
- **The adversarial candidates are hand-written probes, not a sample.** The count of adversarial cases that slip past the obligation grader is an existence demonstration; it is not a rate and has no confidence interval.
- **Mutations are syntactic, not semantic.** `swap_op`, `invert_membership` and `weaken_guard` explore a neighbourhood of the seeded entry that a textual rewrite can reach. A family whose real-world failure mode needs a restructured expression is under-sampled, so the per-family false-accept rates are not comparable across families.
- **Envelope effects are confounded with prompt length and instruction count.** The three envelopes differ in the output contract and in how much text they add. E1 cannot separate 'format cost' from 'extra instruction cost'.
- **Polarity classification is lexical.** `converse_guard` decides from markers, so a correct expression written in an unusual form can be labelled CONVERSE and vice versa. E3.d logs every expression so the labels can be audited by hand; the rate is only as good as the classifier.
- **Temperature and seed are recorded, not guaranteed.** Vendors may ignore seeds or route to different backends, so a re-run is a new sample, not a replay. No variance decomposition is possible from two or three decodings.
- **Only one serving path was measured.** All calls go through one aggregator with whatever quantisation and routing it applies. The results describe that endpoint, not an underlying model artifact.
- **No causal claim about training.** A converse guard or a symptom-suppression patch is an observable output pattern; nothing here identifies why the model produced it.
